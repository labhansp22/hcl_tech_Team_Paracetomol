## 7. Limitations

- The dataset is synthetic and template-based, so retrieval and classification scores are optimistic; the keyword-trap and out-of-scope sets are the more honest tests.
- Priority labels are rule-derived in the synthetic data.
- LLM results depend on the free-tier model available on the day; every analysis records provider and model.
- LLM latency here includes waiting out the Groq free-tier limit (about 8,000 tokens/min, roughly 3 tickets/min) during a batch run; a single interactive ticket takes about 2-3 s, and cached tickets are instant.
- The LLM sample is small (30 tickets, stratified by subcategory), so one ticket = 3.3 percentage points.
